## Why Nyoma?

Most mail clients are built around cloud sync, data harvesting, and subscription lock-in. Nyoma is different:

- **Local-first** — your mail stays on your machine, always accessible offline
- **Privacy by design** — no telemetry, no cloud dependency, no data leaving your device
- **Made in Bharat** — built with sovereign technology values at its core
- **Fast** — no round trips to a remote server for basic operations
- **Open protocols** — works with any standard IMAP/SMTP mail provider

## The name

*Nyoma* is a town in the Ladakh region of India — chosen for its geographic rootedness and clean pronunciation. Like Ladakh, Nyoma the mail client is built to endure harsh conditions: unreliable networks, slow connections, power cuts. Your mail works regardless.
